# AutoVault

A private, local-first home for everything about your vehicles: mileage, service history, documents, expenses, and reminders. No account, no server, no sign-up. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

## What it does

- **Garage**: keep every car, motorcycle, or scooter you own in one place, with a photo, odometer reading, and next-service target.
- **Timeline**: a running log of fuel fill-ups, services, expenses, and odometer updates for each vehicle.
- **Insights**: mileage trends, running cost per km/mile, and where your money is going, calculated from your own records.
- **Maintenance checklist**: track tyres, brakes, fluids, battery, and more, each with its own service interval.
- **Glovebox**: registration, insurance, PUC, warranty, and invoices, with expiry tracking.
- **Reminders & notifications**: real, on-device alerts before something's due, plus your own custom reminders.
- **Search**: find any vehicle, entry, document, or note instantly.
- **Backup & restore**: export your whole garage to a file (optionally passphrase-encrypted), and bring it back later, on this device or another.
- **Units & currency**: Metric or Imperial, and your preferred currency symbol.
- **Installable**: add it to your phone or desktop like a native app; it works offline.

## Your data, your device

AutoVault has no backend, no analytics, and no account system. Nothing you record ever leaves your browser unless you choose to export a backup yourself. See **Settings → Data & Privacy** in the app for the full picture.

## Found a bug? Want a feature?
